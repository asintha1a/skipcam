GOOD TO KNOW

FAQ

Can I scan a QR code from a screenshot?

Yes. Upload or drag in PNG, JPEG, WebP, GIF, BMP or AVIF images, or paste a screenshot from your clipboard.

Can I scan a browser tab or screen?

Choose Scan screen and select a tab, window or display in your browser's native sharing dialog. SkipCam captures one frame, immediately stops the stream and decodes locally. Browser support varies.

Does SkipCam open decoded links automatically?

No. You can inspect the destination and offline warnings, then choose whether to open it.

Is SkipCam free and open source?

Yes. Both the web app and extension are free. SkipCam is MIT licensed and maintained by Self Graphic Lab. Upstream attribution is in the repository's Attribution and License section.
