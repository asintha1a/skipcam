PRIVACY

Your scans stay on this device.

SkipCam does not send QR images, screen captures or decoded values to a server. Optional web history is stored in this browser only, is off by default, and keeps only eligible public HTTPS homepages.

URL warnings use offline checks. They do not check live threat reports or guarantee a link is safe. Opening a decoded link always takes an explicit click.
