# Organic discovery plan

This is a product-fit plan, not a forecast. No keyword volumes, ranking difficulty, traffic, or competitor backlink metrics were available.

## Audience and differentiated value

The clearest user need is reading a QR code already visible on the same computer: in a saved image, screenshot, or browser display. Another need is creating a useful static QR image, especially a Wi-Fi code. SkipCam's product evidence is the working local scanner and generator, user-approved screen capture that stops after a still frame, and an optional browser extension for direct webpage selection.

## Page focus

The qualitative mapping and competitor observations are in [`KEYWORD_STRATEGY.md`](KEYWORD_STRATEGY.md). Avoid creating more pages until first-party query data or user feedback shows a distinct task. The generator formats share one form; URL, email, telephone, SMS, and contact pages would currently repeat the same interaction.

## 30 / 60 / 90 day sequence

### First 30 days

- Verify `skipcam.selfgraphic.tech` in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools.
- Submit the XML sitemap and inspect the homepage plus scan, generator, Wi-Fi, privacy, and security routes.
- Confirm redirects from old hosts and the trailing-slash policy after deployment.
- Review queries and crawl/index reports once data arrives; do not interpret zero early impressions as proof of no demand.

### Days 31–60

- Improve a page only when query data or support requests show a specific mismatch.
- Test the page in real devices and browsers for scan completion, screen permission handling, and mobile layout.
- Re-run PageSpeed Insights or Lighthouse; compare like-for-like device and page results.
- Gather genuine feedback with consent. Do not add tracking pixels or submit user QR data.

### Days 61–90

- Consolidate pages that overlap or provide no distinct helpful content.
- Expand troubleshooting only with verified product behavior and real recurring problems.
- Check referred visits and app usage only if a privacy-preserving, explicitly approved measurement method is available.
- Record changes and evidence before adjusting titles or page targets.

## Guardrails

Do not promise rankings, AI citations, malware protection, or QR link safety. Do not buy links or generate thin location/device pages. Keep the web app usable without an account and preserve local processing.
