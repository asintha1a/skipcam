# Answer and generative search readiness

SkipCam should be easy to understand from the page itself. It should not use unsupported markup or claim special access to AI answers.

## What the site now does

- Gives each useful route one clear H1, a short answer near the top, and task-specific instructions in crawlable HTML.
- Explains the actual product limits: screen sharing requires a user action and browser prompt; images are processed locally; decoded URLs are not opened automatically; URL checks are offline heuristics.
- Identifies Self Graphic Lab as the maintainer and links to the public source and relevant trust pages.
- Uses factual Organization, WebSite, and WebPage structured data without unsupported ratings, reviews, or claims of official store distribution.
- Keeps the personal history page out of the index and sitemap.

## Do not add

- No `llms.txt` as a supposed Google ranking requirement. Google's current published AI search guidance says there is no special markup, file, or content chunking requirement for AI features.
- No FAQPage rich-result markup for marketing FAQs, no fabricated SoftwareApplication ratings, and no self-awarded trust scores.
- No keyword-stuffed questions, repetitive near-duplicate landing pages, hidden SEO text, or claims that an AI engine cites or recommends SkipCam.

## Editorial standard

Answer the user's task plainly, then describe steps and boundaries. Prefer screenshots captured from the functioning application with a truthful caption. Refresh product claims when behavior changes. Every statement about private/local processing must remain true in the shipped build and deployment.

## Evidence to collect later

Once connected properties are available, review query wording, indexed URLs, canonical selection, crawl errors, rich result reports, and actual impressions. Keep a dated record. Search-result citations, rankings, traffic, and referrals must be reported only from the relevant provider data or direct observation.
